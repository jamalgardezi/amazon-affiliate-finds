Start with the product’s intended use

Read where and how the product is intended to be used. A cleanser, face moisturizer and lip balm serve different purposes. Check the directions and selected variant rather than choosing by a brand name or front-label claim alone.

Read the full ingredient and fragrance details

Use the ingredient list on the current listing or package to compare formulas with products you already use. Check fragrance information and any ingredients you already avoid. Variants of the same product may have different formulas, scents or tints.

Compare texture and packaging

Decide whether you prefer a balm, gel, lotion or cream. Check whether it comes in a tube, pump or jar and how it fits your travel or bathroom setup. For tinted lip products, compare the chosen shade with the finish shown in the listing.

Compare cost per unit, not just the bottle price

Confirm the size in ounces or grams and the number of items in the pack. Divide the total product price by the total quantity when comparing equivalent sizes. Include delivery charges and check whether the offer is a one-time purchase or a subscription.

Check the current offer

Review the selected variant, seller, delivery estimate and return conditions. Read recent reviews for details about texture, scent, packaging and the same variant you intend to buy. Catalog ratings are not a substitute for these checks.

Common buying questions

How do I compare different pack sizes?

Divide the product price by the total quantity in the pack, using the same unit for both products. Include delivery charges and distinguish a one-time offer from a subscription.

Where should I check the ingredient list?

Read the label on the current product package and compare it with the manufacturer or current listing for the exact variant. A product name alone does not identify its full formula.
